UniDic is an external Japanese dictionary asset downloaded by explicit user
action. The National Institute for Japanese Language and Linguistics lists the
contemporary written-language dictionary under the GPL v2.0/LGPL v2.1/New BSD
triple license. This prototype selects the permissive New BSD option. Keep the
exact license, attribution and usage-notice files included in the chosen
UniDic archive when redistributing it commercially.
